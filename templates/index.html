Meet the Creator

Mithu Gowda A

AI & Full-Stack Developer · Smart Agriculture Innovator

SmartFarm AI is a passion project developed by Mithu Gowda A to address real-world challenges faced by Indian farmers. Combining expertise in AI/ML, Multi-Agent Systems, and Web Development, this platform leverages cutting-edge technologies like Groq LLM, Python Flask, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to deliver instant, actionable agricultural insights.

The vision is simple: empower every farmer with intelligent tools that were once only available to large agribusinesses — making precision agriculture accessible to all.
